FAQs

Common questions about relocating with MoveNXT.

Still unsure? Our team will walk you through it.

Talk to a manager
What services does MoveNXT provide?

Workplace mobility (office relocation, industrial relocation, data center migration, 3PL warehousing and customs clearance) and workforce mobility, including household relocation, visa support, automobile and pet logistics.

Do you handle international relocation from India?

Yes. Through our FIDI/FAIM-accredited partner network we relocate households and corporate assets across 120 countries, with your dedicated manager as the single point of contact.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Send an enquiry through the form or email enquiry@movenxt.in. A dedicated manager typically responds within one business day.

How long does an office relocation take?

A small office can usually move in 1 to 2 days. A larger office or campus typically needs 2 to 4 weeks of planning, with the move itself done over a weekend to avoid downtime.

Do you move IT equipment and servers?

Yes. Our trained IT relocation crews disconnect, label, pack, transport and reinstall workstations, servers and network equipment. We also handle full data center migration.

Is my goods insurance covered during the move?

We offer transit insurance for all moves, with cover options depending on the value of your goods. Your manager will explain the options during the survey.
